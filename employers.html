About the service

What does a job recruitment agency in Thailand do?

A recruitment agency in Thailand finds Thai workers for an employer and manages the Thai rules for each hire. When the job is abroad, Thai law requires the agency to hold an overseas recruitment licence from the Department of Employment, and every worker must leave through the official process.

So the first thing to check with any Thai agency is its licence number. Ours is ต.1299/2568.
